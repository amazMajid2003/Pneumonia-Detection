About Pneumonia Detection

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide accessible pneumonia detection using artificial intelligence. We aim to assist healthcare professionals and individuals in early detection of pneumonia through chest X-ray analysis.

How It Works

Our system uses a deep learning model trained on thousands of chest X-ray images to identify patterns associated with pneumonia. When you upload an image, our algorithm analyzes it and provides a detection result with confidence level.

Important Notes

  • This tool is not a substitute for professional medical advice
  • Always consult with a healthcare provider for diagnosis
  • Results should be interpreted by qualified medical professionals
  • The system has an accuracy of approximately 90% (simulated for this demo)
