Pneumonia Detection System

Pneumonia is an infection that inflames the air sacs in one or both lungs. Our system helps detect pneumonia from chest X-ray images using advanced AI technology.

Key Facts About Pneumonia

  • Pneumonia can range from mild to life-threatening
  • It's most serious for infants, young children, and older adults
  • Early detection can significantly improve outcomes
  • Chest X-rays are commonly used for diagnosis
